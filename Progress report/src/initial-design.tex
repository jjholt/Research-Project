\subsubsection*{Deviation from original plan}
The original aim was to investigate the change in forces experienced during vibrotactile feedback caused by the different methods of attaching the prosthetic to bone, namely press-fit and cementing.
It became clear that more emphasis should be given to an overall characterisation of vibration passing through the prosthetic, e.g. explore the positions to place the actuators, the axes to displace, the waveform, frequency ranges, etc.

Essentially this project is the creation of a table of appropriate frequencies and displacements that fit the safe parameters that will be determined in the future.
