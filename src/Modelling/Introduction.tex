The experimental setup, as shown in figure \ref{fig:experimental-setup}, aimed to characterise vibrations propagating through ITAP before any interaction with bone or soft tissue.
It consisted of: \begin{enumerate}
    \item ITAP held in place by a vice at the stem, \SI{20}{mm} away from the collar, allowing only for rotation about the X-axis.
    \item A periodic load applied on the spigot at a given offset (``Load offset''), which defaulted to \SI{1}{mm}. The default load was a cosine function with frequency \SI{100}{Hz}, amplitude \SI{0.2}{mm} and duration of 6 periods.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Simplifications}
In order to reduce computational time, we simplified the model by removing most complex features of ITAP, e.g. fillets, pores in the flange, and the flange.
In first instance, we only modelled interactions with bone, thus it was reasonable to remove the part of the device that interacted with soft tissue.
We accepted that this would introduce some error, and aimed to quantify this after initial modelling.
