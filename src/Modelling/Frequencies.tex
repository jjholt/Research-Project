One way we may clinically want to choose the operational frequency is by comparing the relative movement of each part the device.
In figure \ref{fig:max-displacement-frequency} we can see the maximum displacement of each part relative to the maximum displacement of the spigot.
Remarkably, it shows that the maximum relative displacement we observe is around \SI{700}{Hz}, not around its resonant frequency.
It is worth reminding that the operational frequencies are likely to be in the \SIrange{50}{250}{Hz} range, so these minutiae may be inconsequential.
