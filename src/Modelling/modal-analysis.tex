We defined a clinically relevant frequency bandwidth, therefore we ran a modal analysis to ensure the device was well-behaved for those frequencies.
We observed the first mode of vibration at \SI{934}{Hz}.
Natural resonant frequencies depend only on an object's geometry, therefore this result was only relevant to the \SI{12}{cm} stem length.
For a \SI{16}{cm} stem we saw the first mode at \SI{670}{Hz} and for \SI{8}{cm} stem, \SI{1668}{Hz}.
Despite all of them being above the clinically relevant frequencies, there was merit in characterising the device approaching its resonant frequencies.
Ultimately it informed the decision to limit the simulations to below approximately 900 Hz.
