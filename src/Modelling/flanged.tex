Figure \ref{fig:error-compared-to-flanged} compares the maximum displacements of a more complete, ``flanged'', model (F) and the simplification (S) used so far.
This flanged model is far from ideal, but the comparison presented in figure \ref{fig:error-compared-to-flanged} provides some insight into where we can expect the largest changes as we produce more complex models, namely at the collar.
It is worth highlighting that the displacements observed at the collar are consistently much smaller than everywhere else, and that the flange is positioned around the collar, so it is unsurprising that the relative error at the collar is so much larger than elsewhere.
